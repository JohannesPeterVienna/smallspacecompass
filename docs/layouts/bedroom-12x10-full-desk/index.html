Why it is laid out this way

Same room as the queen layout – 144 × 120 in – but the bed is a full (53 × 75 in) placed lengthways along the bottom-right corner with only its top side open. That side carries a 22 in bedside strip, slightly less than the usual 24 in, because the bed is approached from one side only.

The desk takes the top-left corner with its chair zone pointing down. The dresser stands against the top wall and its drawer zone pointing into the room stays clear of both the desk chair and the bed. A slim bookshelf in the top-right corner uses the wall above the bed.

The door is at the left of the bottom wall and swings in towards the left wall, where nothing stands. The route to the bed passes between the desk chair zone and the end of the bed; this is the narrowest point, and the reason the desk is not wider than 42 in.

One figure in the table falls below 36 in: the route to the window, which squeezes between the desk chair zone and the dresser drawers. With the chair pushed in and the drawers closed the real gap is wider, so this is a pinch you can live with – but it is exactly the kind of compromise the planner is designed to make visible before the furniture arrives.
