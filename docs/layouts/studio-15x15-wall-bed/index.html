15 × 15 ft studio with a queen wall bed

A 225 sq ft studio with a queen wall bed on the left wall. In the day the floor in front of it holds a coffee table; at night the table goes against the wall and the bed folds down without touching the loveseat or the entry door.

Why it is laid out this way

The square room is 180 × 180 in (4.57 × 4.57 m). The wall bed cabinet is 66 in wide and 18 in deep and, when open, projects another 68 in into the room – a total of 86 in from the wall. The loveseat is placed below the bed's open footprint, not in front of it, so the bed never has to be cleared before folding down. Only the coffee table is “day only”.

At night the open bed leaves 94 in between its foot and the bottom wall, enough for the 24 in strip at the foot and a wide route from the door to the bed and to the window. The entry door in the bottom-right corner swings in towards the table area, well away from the bed.

Compare the two free-floor figures: the day state keeps over three-quarters of the floor clear; at night the bed takes about one-sixth of it. The Day/Night Floor Swap shows both states side by side, and the Murphy bed room-size guide explains how to size the room around a wall bed.
