22 × 12 ft long, narrow studio in three zones

Long, narrow studios work best as a sequence of zones along one side, with the walkway kept straight along the other. Here the desk is nearest the door, the living area in the middle and the bed at the far window end.

Why it is laid out this way

The room is 264 × 144 in (6.71 × 3.66 m), or 264 sq ft, with the entry in the short left wall and the window in the short right wall. A narrow room tempts you to place furniture on both long walls, which turns the middle into a corridor that is too narrow for anything. This plan keeps the large pieces against the top wall and leaves the bottom half of the room as the main route.

The queen bed is turned so that its long side runs along the top wall at the window end; its free side faces into the room with a 24 in bedside strip. The sofa sits in the middle against the same wall, with the coffee table in front and the TV unit opposite against the bottom wall. That arrangement makes the TV unit the one piece that narrows the route; its 16 in depth is the reason the walkway still measures well above 36 in.

The desk sits near the entry, in the top-left corner. Working nearest the door keeps the sleeping end quiet and means a visitor never has to walk past the bed. The wardrobe is on the bottom wall opposite the bed; its 20 in door-swing zone points up towards the bed and overlaps nothing.
