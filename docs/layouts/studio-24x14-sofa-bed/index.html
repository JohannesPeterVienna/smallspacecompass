Why it is laid out this way

The room measures 288 × 168 in (7.32 × 4.27 m), 336 sq ft. The sofa bed is 78 × 36 in closed and grows by about 54 in when opened; the coffee table in front of it is marked as day only. Everything else stays put: desk and bookshelf in the top-left corner, a table for four in the bottom-right corner, and a TV unit against the bottom wall.

The trade-off of a sofa bed is time and bedding storage, not floor space – see wall bed vs. sofa bed. Here, the open bed still leaves a wide route from the door to the desk and to the bed itself; the window to the left of the sofa stays reachable, although its route is the tightest in the plan because it passes between the desk area and the sofa – the table above shows by how much.

The entry door is at the bottom-left and swings in towards the desk corner. The desk chair zone is positioned so that it ends before the door swing begins, which is why the desk is pushed against the top wall rather than placed beside the door.
