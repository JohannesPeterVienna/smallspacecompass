16 × 20 ft studio with a kitchen wall

When the kitchen is a single run of cabinets along one wall, the work aisle in front of it is part of the room. This plan keeps a 42 in aisle in front of the counters and arranges bed, table and sofa along the opposite wall.

Why it is laid out this way

The room is 192 × 240 in (4.88 × 6.10 m), 320 sq ft, entered from the bottom wall. The kitchen run along the right wall is 120 in long and 25 in deep, a standard counter depth. In front of it the plan reserves a 42 in work aisle – the width the NKBA Kitchen Planning Guidelines recommend for one cook (guideline 6). It is a walk-through zone: the route may pass through it, furniture may not stand in it.

The full bed is in the top-left corner, as far from the cooking area as the room allows. Below it, the table for two has its chair zone pointing towards the kitchen, so the person sitting there faces into the room. The sofa runs along the bottom wall, next to the entry, with a bookshelf beside the door.

The table's chair zone and the kitchen work aisle face each other across the middle of the room; the space between them is what the route uses, and it still measures comfortably above 36 in because both zones were sized with that route in mind. See Small kitchen clearances for the dishwasher and door rules that apply inside the aisle.
