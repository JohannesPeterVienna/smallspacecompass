Day/Night Floor Swap

A wall bed, sofa bed or drop-leaf table changes the room twice a day. This tool shows the same plan in both states and runs the full clearance check on each – so the coffee table that is fine at noon does not end up under the mattress at midnight.

How the two states work

Every piece has a state setting. “Day + night” pieces stay where they are. “Day only” pieces are folded or moved away at night – a coffee table that goes against the wall, a folding chair that hangs on a hook. “Night only” pieces appear only at night, such as a folding guest bed. Fold-away presets also carry an unfold depth: a queen wall bed is about 18 in deep when closed and projects roughly another 68 in into the room when open; a sofa bed grows by the length of its mattress frame. The planner adds that extension on the side you choose and checks the room again.

In each state the same analysis runs as in the Clearance Planner: overlaps, door swing conflicts, blocked use zones and the widest route from the entry to the bed, desk, table and windows, with its narrowest point. A conflict that only appears at night is shown only in the night drawing.

The night-time checks that matter most

  • The door must still open. If the open bed sits inside the swing of the entry door, the planner marks it red. A bed that blocks the only exit is a safety problem, not just an inconvenience.
  • A route to the bed and the window. At night you want to reach the bed without climbing over furniture, and the window should stay reachable.
  • Bedside space. The open bed carries a 24 in walk-through zone at its foot by default; reduce it if you accept a tighter squeeze.
  • Nothing left under the bed. Pieces marked “day only” disappear at night; anything else in the bed's footprint is a conflict.

Frequently asked questions

How much room does a queen wall bed need when it is open?

Roughly 86 in from the wall (about 18 in cabinet plus the open bed) for a vertical queen, plus whatever space you want to walk past its foot. Check the manufacturer sheet for your model.

Can I use it for a normal room without fold-away furniture?

Yes, but the Clearance Planner is simpler for that because it shows a single plan.

Is the plan shared with the Clearance Planner?

No. Each tool keeps its own plan in your browser so that you can work on both. Use JSON export and import to move a plan between them.
