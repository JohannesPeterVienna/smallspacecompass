Tools

Planning tools that check movement, not just footprints

All four tools run entirely in your browser. Plans are kept on your device only and can be exported as SVG or JSON.

Which tool answers which question

Use the Clearance Planner when you are arranging a room: it draws furniture as measured rectangles, adds door swings and the space a chair, drawer or wardrobe door needs, then finds the widest route from the entry to the bed, desk and window and measures its narrowest point. Use Will It Fit? before you order a sofa, mattress or wardrobe: it tests the item straight, on its side, on end and tilted through your door, around a 90° hallway corner and over a stair landing. The Day/Night Floor Swap runs the planner's checks twice for wall beds, sofa beds and drop-leaf tables, once folded and once open. The Storage Wall Audit splits the volume of shelves and cabinets into what sits below 15 in, within the 15–48 in reach range and above it.

All tools work in inches or centimeters and convert exactly. Measure the inside faces of walls and the clear width of doors with the door open 90°; the room measuring guide shows where. The tools are geometric aids: they check rectangles, arcs and heights, not building codes, structural loads or how a piece is carried. Each tool page explains its method, its sources and its limits below the tool.

A sensible order

Before a move or a furniture purchase, the tools work best in this order. First measure the room and the doors, using the measuring guide. Then check every large piece with Will It Fit? – a sofa that cannot get through the hallway corner does not need a place in the plan. Next, arrange the pieces that passed in the Clearance Planner, or in the Day/Night Floor Swap if something folds. Finally, use the Storage Wall Audit to see whether the storage you planned is within easy reach or mostly above your head.

A worked example

Say you are moving into a 20 × 15 ft studio with a 32 in entry door and want to bring a 84 in sofa, a queen bed and a desk. In Will It Fit? you enter the sofa's width, depth and height and the door and hallway measurements; the result says whether it passes straight, on its side or only tilted, and where it gets stuck if it does not. In the Clearance Planner you then open the measured 20 × 15 ft studio layout, change the sofa to 84 in and see the route widths update: if a route drops below the 36 in reference, the marker shows which two pieces cause it. Exporting the plan as SVG gives you a drawing to take to the shop.

What the tools do not check

  • Building rules. Egress, fire and accessibility codes for your building are not part of the checks; the 36 in ADA figure is used as a reference line only.
  • Loads and fixings. Whether a wall can carry a shelf or a wall bed, or a floor a piano, is a question for the building owner or a structural engineer.
  • Carrying. The delivery check tests geometry. It does not know how heavy a piece is, whether the movers can tilt it or whether its legs come off.
  • Odd shapes. Furniture is drawn as rectangles and rooms as rectangles with doors and windows. L-shaped rooms and curved pieces need to be approximated.

Everything you enter stays in your browser. Each tool keeps its own plan in local storage, and you can move a plan between the planner and the swap tool with JSON export and import.

Clearance Planner

Free room planner that measures the narrowest walkway from your door to the bed, desk and window, draws door swings and chair, drawer and wardrobe zones, and flags every conflict.

Will It Fit? Delivery Check

Check whether a sofa, mattress or wardrobe can be carried through your door, around a 90° hallway corner and up a stair landing – straight, tilted or turned – with a drawing of the move.

Tool

Day/Night Floor Swap

Plan fold-away furniture twice: the planner checks your room with the wall bed or sofa bed folded and open, and flags anything that blocks the bed, the door or the walkway at night.

Tool

Storage Wall Audit

Draw a storage wall, add shelves and cabinets and see how much volume sits within easy reach (15–48 in), above it and below it – plus an equal-compartment divider calculator.

Measured Layout Library
