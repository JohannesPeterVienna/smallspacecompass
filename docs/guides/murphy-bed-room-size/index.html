Murphy bed room size: how much space a wall bed needs

A wall bed needs three things: enough wall, enough ceiling height and enough clear floor in front when it is down. Here is how to size each one for a queen, full or twin, and what has to stay free at night.

Last reviewed: October 20265 min readUnits: in + cm

Three dimensions to check

Wall beds differ by manufacturer, so the numbers below are planning figures based on the mattress size plus a cabinet allowance. Mattress sizes are the U.S. standard sizes listed in Casper’s size chart. Always confirm with the manufacturer's installation sheet for the exact model.

The approximations add about 6 in to the mattress for the cabinet sides and about 6–18 in to the depth for the cabinet and frame. A vertical wall bed (head against the wall) needs a wall slightly wider than the mattress and a ceiling higher than the bed length plus the cabinet top – typically around 84–90 in for a queen. A horizontal wall bed (long side against the wall) needs a wider wall but less height and less floor depth, which helps in rooms with low ceilings or sloping roofs.

What has to stay free in front

When the bed is down, the floor it covers must be empty – no coffee table, no rug with furniture on it, no plant on the floor. On top of that, plan:

  • A 24 in strip at the foot (this site's bedside assumption) to walk past and make the bed.
  • A route from the entry door to the bed at least 36 in wide – the clear width in ADA 2010 §403.5.1 – which may use the strip at the foot.
  • The entry door must open fully with the bed down. A bed inside the door swing is a safety problem.

For a queen vertical wall bed, that means about 86 + 24 = 110 in (just over 9 ft) of room depth in front of the bed wall, plus whatever the walkway needs if it does not use the foot strip.

What can stand beside it

The wall bed's own side panels can usually take shelves or a desk unit (many systems offer them). Freestanding furniture beside the cabinet is fine as long as it does not stand where the open bed lands. A sofa or loveseat placed alongside the open bed's footprint – rather than in front of the cabinet – is the arrangement that works best day to day, because nothing has to move except a light coffee table.

The wall itself

  • Most wall beds must be anchored to the wall structure (studs or masonry); some have a freestanding frame. Ask your landlord before drilling.
  • Check for outlets, switches, radiators, skirting heaters and windows on the bed wall – the cabinet will cover them.
  • Check the floor in front: a door threshold or a change of floor level within the open footprint can prevent the bed from resting flat.

Step-by-step sizing

  1. Pick the mattress size and orientation (vertical or horizontal).
  2. Measure the wall width and ceiling height at the bed wall; compare with the manufacturer's minimums.
  3. Measure from the bed wall to the opposite wall or furniture: open depth + 24 in strip at least.
  4. Draw the entry door swing and check that the open bed is outside it.
  5. Check the route from the door to the bed and the window at night.
  6. Check delivery: wall bed cabinets come flat-packed or in large parts – check the largest box with the delivery check.

Frequently asked questions

How much space do you need for a queen Murphy bed?

A wall about 66 in wide, roughly 86 in of floor in front of the wall when open (for a vertical bed), plus about 24 in at the foot to walk past. Ceiling height depends on the model, often around 84–90 in.

What is the difference between vertical and horizontal wall beds?

A vertical bed folds up with its head against the wall: narrower wall, deeper floor, higher ceiling. A horizontal bed folds with its long side against the wall: wider wall, less depth and height.

Can you put furniture in front of a Murphy bed?

Only pieces you move away each evening. Anything in the open footprint must be cleared before the bed comes down.

Can a Murphy bed go in a 10x10 room?

A full or queen vertical wall bed fits a 10 × 10 ft room, leaving about 34 in (queen) at the foot when open.
