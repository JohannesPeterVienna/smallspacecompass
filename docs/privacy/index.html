Privacy policy

This policy explains what data is processed when you visit smallspacecompass.com, why, and what rights you have. Last updated: November 5, 2026.

Controller

AdStern, Seilerstätte 8, 1010 Vienna, Austria (full details in the imprint).

Hosting

The site is a static website hosted on GitHub Pages, a service of GitHub, Inc., 88 Colin P. Kelly Jr. Street, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA. When you load a page, your browser sends technical data such as your IP address, the requested address, date and time, browser type and referring page to GitHub's servers. GitHub processes this data to deliver the site and to protect its service, and may store it in log files for a limited time. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in a secure and reliable website (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). GitHub's own privacy statement is available at docs.github.com. Data transfers to the USA are based on the EU–U.S. Data Privacy Framework, under which GitHub is certified, or on standard contractual clauses.

Your plans and inputs

Everything you enter into the planning tools – room sizes, furniture, door positions – is processed only in your own browser. Plans are saved in your browser's local storage so they are still there on your next visit; they are not sent to us or to anyone else. You can delete them at any time with the tool's reset button or by clearing your browser's site data. Files you export or import are handled entirely on your device.

Advertising (Google AdSense)

This site shows advertising from Google AdSense, a service of Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland (for users in the EEA and UK) and Google LLC, USA (elsewhere). AdSense uses cookies and similar technologies to show ads, limit how often you see them, measure their performance and prevent fraud.

Guide, tool, layout and overview pages (the home page, the indexes and About) can show advertising; Google places these ads automatically, so their number and position can vary. The imprint, this privacy policy, the contact page and the error page load no advertising code at all and show no ads. Google's consent message is loaded there only when you click “Open Google's ad & U.S. privacy choices” in the window behind “Privacy settings”; only Google's consent script is loaded then, not the ad script.

Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this website or other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visits to this and/or other sites on the internet.

You can opt out of personalized advertising in Google's Ads Settings. You can also opt out of the use of cookies by third-party vendors for personalized advertising at www.aboutads.info or, in Europe, at www.youronlinechoices.eu. More information on how Google uses data is available at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Consent. For visitors in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, a consent message from Google's certified consent management platform (IAB Transparency & Consent Framework) asks for your choices before personalized ads or cookies that require consent are used. The legal basis is your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR, § 165(3) Austrian Telecommunications Act). Personalized ads require your consent. Non-personalized ads (based on page content and approximate location) also use cookies or similar identifiers, for example for frequency capping, aggregated reporting and fraud prevention, so they too are shown only if you consent to storing and accessing information on your device. Without that consent Google can show only limited ads. Limited ads are not personalised and do not use cookies or local storage for personalised advertising; Google may still use technical data, and where permitted storage, for fraud prevention and frequency limits as described in its documentation. You can change or withdraw your consent at any time with the link, which is also in the footer of every page.

Analytics (Google Analytics 4)

We use Google Analytics 4 to understand which guides and tools are used, so we can improve them. Before any Google script runs, Google Consent Mode is set to “denied” for advertising and analytics storage and Google Analytics is disabled. While Google's consent message is not displayed, Google Analytics is loaded only after you have allowed it in our interim statistics window (see Interim statistics window below). Otherwise, in the EEA, UK and Switzerland, Google Analytics is loaded only after you have given consent in the consent message for storing and accessing information on your device (TCF purpose 1), for Google as a vendor and for analytics. Outside those regions it is loaded only if the consent message reports analytics as granted or as not requiring consent for you, and has confirmed that you have not opted out under a U.S. state privacy law; missing or unclear signals keep it off. If you withdraw consent through “Privacy settings”, Consent Mode is set back to “denied” and Google Analytics is disabled immediately. Google signals and ad personalization are switched off in our setup. If your browser sends a Global Privacy Control signal, Google Analytics stays off. The imprint, this privacy policy, the contact page and the error page never load Google Analytics. IP addresses are not stored by Google Analytics 4. Event data is stored for the retention period set in our Google Analytics property and then deleted. The legal basis in Europe is your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR). Data may be transferred to Google LLC in the USA under the EU–U.S. Data Privacy Framework.

Interim statistics window

Google's consent message is set up for this site but is currently not displayed. So that visits are still only counted with your consent, we show our own small window for statistics while this is the case. It appears about three seconds after a page has loaded if Google's consent management has not reported a decision by then, is not showing its own message and does not report that the GDPR does not apply to your visit. It asks only about statistics with Google Analytics 4 (Google Ireland Limited). It is not a consent to advertising: it only ever changes the Consent Mode setting for analytics storage, while the settings for advertising storage, ad user data and ad personalization stay “denied”. “Allow” and “Decline” are equal choices. Until you choose, Google Analytics stays off; closing the window with “×” or the Escape key stores nothing and is not consent: without a stored choice, statistics stay off and we ask again on your next page view (not again on the same page); a choice you stored earlier stays in place.

What is stored. Your choice is saved in your browser's local storage under the key ssc-stats, with the content “granted” or “denied”, a version number and the time of your choice. This entry is needed to respect your decision and is not sent to us or to Google. After 12 months, or if we change the window, we ask again. If you allow statistics, Google Analytics loads as described above and sets cookies on your device (_ga, _ga_…); data may be transferred to Google LLC in the USA under the EU–U.S. Data Privacy Framework. If you decline or later withdraw, measurement stops at once, Google Analytics is switched off and the Google Analytics cookies (_ga, _ga_…) set for this domain are deleted.

Legal basis. Your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR; for storing and reading information on your device § 165(3) Austrian Telecommunications Act 2021, TKG 2021). You can change or withdraw your choice at any time with the link at the bottom of every page, including this one. On the imprint, this privacy policy, the contact page and the error page it always opens this window. Whenever the window is opened through “Privacy settings”, it also offers a button “Open Google's ad & U.S. privacy choices”, which opens Google's consent message so you can withdraw an advertising consent or opt out under a U.S. state privacy law; on pages with advertising code it uses the consent tool already loaded, on the pages named above Google's consent script is loaded only on your click. This button never gives an advertising consent by itself. Google Analytics stays off on the imprint, privacy policy, contact page and error page in any case.

Google's consent management comes first. As soon as Google's consent message is displayed, our window closes and is no longer shown on that page. As soon as Google's consent management reports a decision, or reports that the GDPR does not apply to your visit, only Google's settings count and a choice stored in our window is deleted. If you do not consent to analytics in Google's consent message, the Google Analytics cookies are deleted, whether or not you used our window before. If your browser sends a Global Privacy Control signal, Google Analytics stays off, the window is not shown and any Google Analytics cookies (_ga, _ga_…) for this domain are deleted.

Visitors in the United States. Where Google's consent management reports that the GDPR does not apply to your visit, our window is not shown and Google Analytics follows the rules in the analytics section above, including your opt-out under a U.S. state privacy law; an opt-out reported by Google's consent management also keeps Google Analytics off while our window is in use, and an opt-out reported through the IAB Global Privacy Platform deletes the Google Analytics cookies.

If you write to us, we use your email address and message only to answer you, and delete them when they are no longer needed for that purpose, unless legal retention periods apply (Art. 6(1)(b) and (f) GDPR).

Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right of access (Art. 15), rectification (Art. 16), erasure (Art. 17), restriction of processing (Art. 18), data portability (Art. 20) and to object to processing based on legitimate interests (Art. 21). Where processing is based on consent, you can withdraw it at any time with effect for the future, for example with the Privacy settings link.

Because we do not store any personal data about you ourselves, most requests concern Google; Google's privacy controls are available at myaccount.google.com.

You have the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority. In Austria this is the Datenschutzbehörde (Austrian Data Protection Authority), Barichgasse 40–42, 1030 Vienna, Austria, www.dsb.gv.at. You can also contact the authority in your own country of residence.

Notice for U.S. residents

Some U.S. state privacy laws, including the California Consumer Privacy Act as amended by the CPRA, treat the use of advertising cookies for cross-context behavioral advertising as “selling” or “sharing” personal information. Where these laws apply, Google's consent management platform shows a “Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information” message on this site, through which you can opt out. You can open it again at any time via the Privacy settings link. We do not sell personal information in any other way, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 16.
